# Explore a concept without forcing a connection

The receipt's `value` is the exact drawn concept. It is context for exploration, not a claim that the concept must fit the user's problem. Start from relational properties: what changes, accumulates, constrains, depends on or responds to something else? Use the drawn lens to choose where to look first.

## Bounded procedure

1. **Cast three to five plausible properties.** Distinguish sourced donor facts from remembered assumptions and hypothetical constructions. Verify a technical causal claim when the proposed move depends on it. Do not use surface nouns as evidence.
2. **Probe the frozen brief.** Ask what each property suggests: an intervention, a failure mode to check, a constraint, a measurement or a next question. Name the actual target particulars and the transfer's limits. Similarity does not prove equivalence.
3. **Release weak or duplicate strands.** Keep a direction only if the donor explanation is defensible and a relevant concrete action survives removal. The target action still needs its own correctness and feasibility check.
4. **Refine once.** A promising but loose strand gets one adjacent association, followed by the same checks. Bounded refinement is not permission to search until an analogy can be asserted.

A concept with a relevant professional field can be considered through a simulated practitioner perspective; an everyday object need not be turned into a person. Do not invent experts, citations or a theoretical explanation of what happened inside the model.

## A grounded form for an uncertain donor

Suppose the cue is adenosine and the brief concerns retries and a queue. Do not assume a simplified sleep-pressure story is sufficient evidence for the software system. Do not claim that API endpoints cannot saturate or that every rate limiter hides accumulated backlog.

A defensible path is to verify any relevant donor physiology separately, then phrase the target idea as a hypothesis: “If retries accumulate faster than this service can drain them, changing the rate-limit threshold may leave that accumulation unresolved.” Inspect the actual queue and retry behavior before treating the condition as true.

A possible move is to compare arrival, retry and completion counts over the same observed window and test a bounded retry change in the user's existing environment. Use the real available measurements and stated constraints. If the brief supplies no queue or retry data, do not invent them; offer a scoped inspection or release the strand. No positive outcome, literal isomorphism or novel mechanism has been established by the analogy.

## Present the useful result

Offer only the few directions that survive, with a short donor noticing, explicit transfer hypothesis and concrete optional move plus check. Label proposed calibration values and unresolved assumptions. Do not force three outputs or describe abstention as a failure to try hard enough.

If asked about discarded alternatives, give concise decision summaries: what was considered and which constraint or unsupported premise caused it to be dropped. Do not claim to expose a full private reasoning transcript. Saving selected moves and their receipt remains opt-in under the main skill.
