# PRODUCT.md - wildcard

register: brand

## Product purpose
Landing page for "wildcard", an experimental Claude Code skill that draws a random wildcard using
real OS entropy - either a hyper-specific niche specialist (378 disciplines) or a general concept
(461 concepts) - to seed non-linear creative thinking in LLMs. The wildcard is a seed, not a
subject: it steers thinking somewhere new rather than being forced to map onto the problem.

The page is a portfolio piece: it must DEMONSTRATE the thesis, not just describe it. The page
itself is a seeded draw, dealt onto a measured golden-ratio grid, different every visit and
reproducible from any seed in the visitor's own terminal.

## Users
Design-literate engineers, AI practitioners, and potential collaborators or employers viewing a
portfolio. Curious, unhurried, on laptops/desktops in daylight. They will fact-check claims;
nothing on the page may be simulated or faked. Assume they check one number, and that whichever
one they check has to hold.

## Brand and tone
Playful-but-rigorous. Lowercase, calm, honest. Nature-as-mechanism (pollination, germination,
outbreeding, stochastic resonance) without nature kitsch. Every "live" element is real: real
entropy, real session transcripts, seed parity with the actual shell script, falsifiable from a
terminal.

Honesty is the differentiator, not a disclaimer. The study that went against the product is
published as prominently as the one that supported it, and the front-door figures panel leads
with both.

## Strategic principles
- Medium matches message, but only if real. No faked demos, no decorative claims.
- The modular grid IS the design language (measured from the user's own Chickpea generative
  system output). Modular yet coherent: many moving parts, one piece.
- Single viewport at desktop. Content lives in cells, never appended below the fold.
- Navigation IS reshuffling: changing view re-deals the page.
- Every claim on the page names the command that regenerates it.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing pages (hero-metric template, identical card grids, gradient text).
- Em dashes anywhere in copy.
- Long scroll storytelling pages.
- Fake "AI magic" demos that cannot be reproduced.
- Placeholder or scaffolding copy surviving into production.
