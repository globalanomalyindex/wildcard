# PRODUCT.md - wildcard

register: brand

## Product purpose

Wildcard is an experimental Claude Code skill for exploring an outside perspective. It draws a niche specialist or concept, examines possible relations to a frozen brief, and offers optional concrete moves. The sampler selects the cue; the model generates and checks the proposed transfer. This is inference-time context conditioning, without weight changes or claims about inaccessible internal mechanisms.

The site is a portfolio piece by christopher robin fiore, developed with AI assistance. It should demonstrate the actual local draw and make the product's evaluation story inspectable. A live cue selector is not live model reasoning or evidence of improved creativity. Recorded examples must be labeled and linked to source artifacts.

## Users and setting

Design-literate engineers, AI practitioners and potential collaborators or employers examining work on laptops/desktops in daylight, with a usable mobile reading path. They should quickly find what was built, what changed after testing, what failed and how to reproduce a claim. Do not assume they will read every paragraph before noticing a limitation.

## Brand and tone

Playful, calm and specific. Preserve the orange/pale/ink palette, unusual wordmark and measured grid. Lowercase is an editorial voice; exact seeds, code, scientific notation, model identifiers and proper names must remain unaltered where fidelity matters. Nature can inspire the visual language, but a biological metaphor must not be presented as a causal model of an LLM.

Honesty is a product behavior: stable visible draws, exact receipts, optional suggestions, source-linked examples, observable checks and a result that remains visible when unfavorable. Do not use “guaranteed safety,” “validated creativity” or perfect procedural-isolation claims.

## Strategic principles

- Preserve a compact authored desktop composition and a clear stacked layout when space is constrained.
- Let the user control draws. Sharing, copying and the displayed receipt must describe the same completed result.
- Keep browsing study evidence separate from issuing a new draw; navigation should not silently discard a result.
- Show the problem, cue, proposed move and its limit in a recorded example. Do not simulate inference.
- Pair estimates with their comparison, unit, uncertainty and source. Label historical results, new audits and proposed studies separately.
- Keep the evidence that changed the design as accessible as the evidence that favored it.
- Attribute the work to christopher robin fiore; GitHub and package namespace remain globalanomalyindex.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS hero metrics, repeated icon cards, gradient text, decorative dashboards, unrelated redesign palettes and invented AI activity. The landing page should not become an undifferentiated long-scroll report. The case study may use a navigable reading layout. No filler copy, unsupported guarantees or invisible interaction state.
