## angles
- Customer "not home" failures mirror restaurant no-shows (10-20% before fixes, solved by changing commitment) -> both are irreversible-commitment problems where prediction isn't the lever -> reconfirmation text 2 hours before stop + nearby-customer standby list to swap no-shows instead of dead mileage

- "Will sofa fit" and "elevator reserved" are solved in surgery (pre-anesthesia implant check) and aviation (pre-pushback checklist) by moving verification earlier and assigning it explicitly -> both moved verification from implicit/at-door to explicit/earlier with clear ownership -> mandatory booking-time video call or stairwell photo + system block on freight-elevator buildings until reservation confirmed

- 4-hour windows create cliff expectations; delivery apps replaced fixed windows with live narrowing numbers ("14 away" → "3 away") -> continuous updates prevent expectation breaks at deadline -> GPS-triggered "3rd in line, ETA 3:40" texts might convert 2pm complaint spike to non-event (most complaints are about surprise, not lateness)

- Rental cars and Airbnb use mandatory timestamped photos at both ends of handoff to create evidence instead of he-said-she-said -> shifts damage/delivery liability to documented record -> require stairwell/doorway photos (before/after) in crew app before close-out, like rental return photo requirement

- Peak-weekend crew shortage is the pool problem Instawork/TaskRabbit exist to solve -> on-call pre-vetted labor for spike volume instead of permanent bench strength -> tap gig labor for weekend peaks if recurring cost justifies it
