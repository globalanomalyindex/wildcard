## angles
- Simultaneous waste and shortage on same day -> textbook signature of mismatched service level per SKU, not bad luck -> set deliberate stocking ratio per cafe based on cost of waste vs. shortage; check last 8-12 Saturdays for pattern of chronically shorted cafes
- Orders trickling in until 10pm, then 11pm mix start -> system with no controlled airspace; first-come-first-served chaos through production window -> hard cutoff time made visible and cafe-facing ("orders locked at 8pm, standing order unless changed") rather than internal constraint
- Two ovens competing for same recovery space at same hours -> structurally identical to hospital services sharing recovery ward -> block scheduling with reserved, non-overlapping windows per process; sometimes the fix is moving when a process runs, not expanding the room
- Head baker keeps schedule in his head and wants to cut back nights -> single point of failure with unplanned knowledge-transfer deadline -> shadow him specifically to externalize his heuristics (why croissant gets priority over bread on Fridays, etc.) before the constraint tightens
