## angles
- Pyrolysis is staged, not one-step (good charcoal requires sequential moisture and volatile gas removal before charring under restricted oxygen) -> Delivery failures (not-home, won't-fit, no-elevator) are discovered mid-route in the same step as delivery, equivalent to combining cure and char in one burn -> Split verification from execution into two owners and timings: T-24h dispatch pass (by dispatch, before route loading) confirms customer reachable, freight elevator booked with building management, and stairwell or doorway clearance checked against item dimensions; only stops that clear get routed
- Charcoal never bonds and just sits on surface by friction, requiring immediate fixing in light passes rather than one blanket coat applied after smudging -> Stairwell scuffs cannot be separated old wear from new because the ambiguity was already set before documentation -> Timestamped photos at chokepoints (stairwell turns, doorframes, elevator interior) taken on arrival before any contact and on departure, filed against stop manifest; record exists before contact happens at points where scuffs would occur
- Activated charcoal is manufactured on a standardized schedule through distinct processing (steam or chemical), not found or produced in response to demand -> Peak-weekend shortage of vetted crew stems from hunting for people who already have the needed quality instead of running the process that produces it -> Run standing weekly half-day activation pass (scored checklist covering elevator and stairwell handling, tie-down technique, and one supervised live delivery) for temp-agency and seasonal labor ahead of forecasted peak dates to build a small reserve of activated floaters on schedule
