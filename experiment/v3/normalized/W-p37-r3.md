## angles
- Temperature reading matters at friction zone (blades), not at gauge or center probe, because local overheating happens there -> reefer vans read fine where measured (jacket), but excursions occur at dock morning sort where no instrument exists -> place continuous logger on dock sort table logging every 1-2 minutes from truck-open to van-load
- Once lots mix in vat they can't unmix; lot ID written when delivery enters mill, never after -> traceability problem sits in afterward with lot data on paper invoices decoupled from box; rejection requires reconstructing -> put sticker on each box at dock crossing with supplier, landing date, sort-batch code
- Quality gates on time-and-temperature dose, not clock alone; paste that ran hot needs earlier pressing than paste that stayed cool -> day-2 discount is flat clock applied to all boxes regardless of cold-chain history; zero-excursion and multi-excursion boxes treated identically -> split day-2 handling on dock log: zero-excursion boxes get longer window, excursion-touched boxes flagged for same-day sale or write-off before day 2
