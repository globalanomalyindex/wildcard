## angles
- Unanchored counts drift invisibly; thickness compacts unevenly with depth, error accumulates unless re-anchored to independent markers -> printed manifest is a single 7am read trusted to hold eight hours with no re-anchoring to actual road conditions; gap compounds invisibly until surfacing as missed afternoon windows -> pick 2-3 fixed checkpoints per route where crews text actual elapsed time versus planned; dispatch recomputes and pushes updated afternoon windows before gap compounds
- In chronology, only layers with two independent blind reads count; sampling bias emerges when scrutinizing only suspected anomalies -> the 3% damage-rate figure may measure suspicion not actual rate; only stairwell photos exist from spots already suspected problematic, leaving disputes as competing accounts with nothing independent to check -> require timestamped photos of stairwell walls (before and after touching furniture) on 100% of stairwell deliveries, not just risky ones; gives unbiased read and turns disputes into contemporaneous record
- Appearance lies constantly; a layer can look annual until actually measured at that specific spot; a number taken at location is data, a glance is not -> "won't fit the stairwell" usually means nobody measured there; someone eyeballed or trusted customer's phone guess -> for items over a size threshold, require real measurements on file before dispatch (stairwell width, landing turn radius, ceiling height) taken by phone tape measure or video at pre-delivery confirmation call; no measurement on file, no truck loaded
