## angles
- Engineers use representative sensors instead of inspecting everything -> you can't audit 40 accounts individually -> pull a credit report's address-history section to surface forgotten accounts without manual audit
- Damage accumulates invisibly from the start and queue slots vanish if dates shift -> three-week ISP backlog seems safe against five-week move but losing the slot costs the full three weeks -> place ISP order today on best-guess date to hold the slot; date adjustments are one call, queue loss is irreversible
- Sacrificial connection protects downstream by absorbing upstream corrosion -> passport already in flight to old address is a signal you can't update accounts for -> file USPS mail-forwarding order today as the first move since it's the only lever for items already in flight
- Dielectric union breaks the circuit when unremovable things at different potential contact -> you and offices share the same 9-to-6 circuit so you can't out-schedule live calls -> check online forms, portals, email first before assuming you must call; reserve live calls for things that structurally require voice
