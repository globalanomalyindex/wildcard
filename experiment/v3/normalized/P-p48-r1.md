## angles
- Account updates have different constraint shapes (some time-locked, most flexible) -> you're treating them as one urgent pile -> sort by "hard external clock" (passport, ISP, lease) vs. "just eventually" and plan five weeks around the first
- Indirection layer catches all references instead of updating each call site -> instead of updating 40 accounts before moving, establish one buffer -> file USPS mail forwarding early as a 12-month catch-all; update accounts whenever
- Old and new systems run in parallel during a cutover window -> the 6-day overlap feels like pressure not opportunity -> move big stuff early in the window, keep old place minimally live, dual-service utilities through ISP backlog
- Most updates are two-way doors; appointment slots are one-way doors with no lateness margin -> your limited business-hours time is being spent indiscriminately -> identify which calls require live humans (ISP, maybe DMV) and move everything else to async channels
