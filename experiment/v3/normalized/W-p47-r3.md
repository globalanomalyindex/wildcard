## angles
- A medical flowsheet requires that every change (humidity setting) be charted with the vitals that justified it; without the "why," the value survives but becomes orphaned -> Your birth-year dispute is stuck the same way: both values survived but the reasoning behind each didn't -> Create a single source-tracking spreadsheet (Fact | Value | Source | Date entered | Confidence), backfill the contested entry with what you can reconstruct, and require every new fact to carry a source before entry.
- Humidity weaning works in incremental steps (5-10 points every 24-48 hours), each advanced only when numbers confirm the previous step was tolerated; a reversible pause beats a hard cutoff you can't undo -> You've been framing renew-or-cancel as a single binary decision at the subscription deadline -> Spend the remaining four weeks exporting in stages (week 1 full GEDCOM, week 2 resolve pending hints, week 3 download high-res copies), confirm each export is legible at checkpoints, and pause rather than cancel to keep the door open.
- Delaying a necessary step (skin-to-air transition) longer than medically needed creates compounding risk from both directions; similarly, paced interactions prevent fatigue-based failure -> Waiting to identify photos risks losing the people who can do it, but one marathon session risks exhausting everyone before finishing -> Number 10-12 photos in advance, bring only those per visit, cap at 20-30 minutes, and ask one specific question per photo; repeatable short sittings will reach further than one long session.
