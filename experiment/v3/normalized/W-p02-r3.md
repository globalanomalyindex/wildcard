## angles
- Webhook outages are caused by universal coupling to pieces that touch the entire system at once, not workload problems -> The admission webhook intercepts every pod's scheduling decision cluster-wide; its failure causes instant, total, fleet-wide collapse unlike per-workload failures -> Deploy webhook changes with `failurePolicy: Ignore` and short timeout on canary node pool first; require 48-hour observation window before promoting to `Fail` cluster-wide; hold this component's promotion bar categorically stricter than any per-workload rollout
- Stateful services pinned to local PVs cannot be safely detached and moved, just as quantum states cannot be copied or relocated -> Local-PV services need teleportation not relocation: pre-establish destination state, destroy original via verified measurement, send small correction -> For each stateful node-pinned service, before touching the node, stand up replicated destination volume on target node using app-level replication, let it catch up, then execute scripted verified failover with checksum matching
- Eight teams with limited capacity split across three clusters cannot maintain strong correlation on any single migration -> Entanglement is monogamous: split across many partners degrades to weak classical correlation, yielding close to zero value on any one effort -> Name exactly one point of contact per team committed to this migration alone; explicitly freeze their involvement in other two clusters' upgrade work; reject "help as time allows" as known anti-pattern
