## angles
- USPS mail-forwarding was built for exactly this (multiple account updates) -> turns forty separate problems into one forwarding order plus selective account updates at your pace -> file the forwarding order, confirm passport handling separately, update accounts on your schedule
- Triage sorts by irreversibility and time-sensitivity, not inconvenience -> treating all 40 items equally when some can't be undone (passport, movers date) and many can tolerate weeks of delay -> protect 2-3 irreversible items; let everything else be slow
- Supply chains bridge slow resources rather than rushing them -> treating the three-week ISP install backlog as something that must be accelerated -> use mobile hotspot to decouple internet availability from ISP installation; book early and stop treating it as critical path
- Distributed systems solve synchronous coordination via async channels (forms, portals) -> assuming account updates and address changes require live calls during business hours -> audit which of forty items actually need live calls versus have online forms; batch the genuinely stubborn ones into one PTO morning
