## angles
- Dye lots are visually identical but differ subtly and are tracked by lot number and knotted at intervals for traceability -> suppliers' product becomes indistinguishable once mixed on a tray; rejection can only be traced through invoices -> tag every crate with lot and catch-time before sorting, scan at each transfer checkpoint for quick lookup
- Distortion in embroidery shows on the back during unsupported stretches between hoopings, before it shows on the front -> dock staging is an unsupported temperature stretch with no direct monitoring; excursions only surface after rejections -> install continuous logging temperature sensor at dock staging specifically with alarm at 40 degrees for live detection
- Partly used skeins are consciously downgraded to secondary motifs or remnant stock immediately when it's clear they won't serve the original purpose -> inventory currently runs on a single cliff: full price through day 2, then fish market loss; like an undecided remnant degrading in the stash -> build a tiered discount schedule partway through day 2 to move product into secondary channels early, reserving fish market as true last resort
