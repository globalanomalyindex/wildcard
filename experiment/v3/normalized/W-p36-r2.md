## angles
- Fern fronds unfurl only when conditions are confirmed safe, protecting the vulnerable tip -> Current failure modes (no-show, dimension mismatch, no elevator) are discovered at the door at maximum exposure instead of beforehand -> Default stops to "not loaded"; before truck loading, independently confirm same-day occupancy (within 2-hour window), item dimensions against recorded stairwell/doorway width, and booked freight elevator slot; any unconfirmed, hold that day's load.
- A frond grows by repeating one rule at every branch point, not from a pre-drawn master blueprint -> The printed manifest solved once at 6am becomes stale after the first stop runs late, causing afternoon windows to start missing by 2pm -> After each stop completes or fails, re-rank remaining stops by proximity to 4-hour window violation using the same fixed rule; hand the crew the next stop (not the whole day's list) rebuilt live.
- Ferns spread from a shared underground rhizome connecting multiple fronds, so new growth draws on established trusted reserves, not an unconnected seed -> Weekend surge crews are built from scratch under time pressure: rented trucks and unvetted hires with no shared foundation -> Pre-certify a standing roster of 8 to 10 warehouse staff as fill-in second-crew members with the same background check and training as regular crews; draw weekend surge from this internal pool before seeking external help.
- Fern identification works through a fixed set of comparable structural checkpoints (vein branching, pinna attachment angle), checked the same way every time -> Damage claims are unresolvable because there's no fixed, comparable evidence set—just inconsistent snapshots -> Define 4 to 5 fixed photo checkpoints per staircase (tightest turn, doorframe edge, banister post nearest the turn, landing wall corner); require crews to shoot exactly those checkpoints in the same order before and after every job.
