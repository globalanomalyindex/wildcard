## angles
- trauma surgery does minimum to stop bleeding before planned repairs, not everything in one operation -> your deadline is only about unsupported status, not full modernization (two different problems) -> split into "stop the bleeding" (control plane current enough to stay supported) then "definitive repair" (deprecated APIs, PV migration) as unhurried second phase
- ring vaccination targets highest-transmission nodes first because untouched high-risk areas cause outbreaks to restart -> your riskiest workloads (deprecated API + local PV + low team capacity together) should determine sequencing, not be uniform in a 60-workload rollout -> map those patient-zero combinations as their own tier with dedicated attention
- circuit breakers and fuses are designed to fail safely, not toward shutdown; your admission webhook is a single point that can block cluster-wide scheduling by design -> the 40-minute outage showed fail-closed applied globally when one webhook broke -> audit failurePolicy on critical admission webhooks, set to Ignore (fail-open) or scope with namespaceSelector/objectSelector so a bad webhook can only block a namespace
- negotiators don't ask broad cooperation from parties with limited bandwidth, they identify smallest specific commitment per party that unblocks -> most of your eight teams probably don't need to do anything; only the few touching deprecated APIs or local PVs do -> send each team a workload-specific one-line ask instead of general "prepare for upgrade" message; skip teams with nothing to do, since smaller asks usually get faster action
