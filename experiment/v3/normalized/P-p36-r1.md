## angles
- Failures (not home, won't fit stairwell, elevator unavailable) were knowable before the truck left the warehouse -> These are verification-timing problems rather than scheduling problems -> Pre-departure clearance step (confirm window by text, confirm dimensions against stairwell photo, confirm elevator reservation)
- Healthcare and aviation reconfirm hours before departure when circumstances are actually knowable -> Directly targets the "not home" portion of failures with real-time state information -> Same-day 7am robocall or text asking for reconfirmation, cheap to pilot on ten stops before broader rollout
- Insurance adjusters and rental-car companies solved liability disputes with mandatory photo documentation at handoff -> Photo evidence at pickup and drop-off may not reduce actual damage but collapses liability exposure and dispute resolution time -> Mandatory photography walk-around before and after delivery
- Some furniture and moving companies maintain a pre-vetted pool activated on-demand rather than hiring for peak demand -> Decouples vetting from the scheduling crunch timeline -> Move crew vetting off the peak-weekend timeline, maintaining a pre-vetted pool ready for on-demand activation
