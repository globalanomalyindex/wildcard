wildcard is an experimental skill for claude code. at random, it drops something foreign into your session: a hyper-specific specialist from an unrelated field, or a plain concept to think against. the draw is a seed, not a subject. you do not force your problem onto it, you let it pull your thinking somewhere new and keep only what holds up.

an llm's chain of thought never wanders, so the stray idea that pops in from nowhere cannot happen. wildcard is that missing wandering mind, bolted on from the outside.
